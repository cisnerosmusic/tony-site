Books

Fourteen published books, in the order that makes sense from outside Cuba.

Titles are kept in their original Spanish, with their meaning in brackets. Every page carries the back cover, the author's own note on the book, excerpts in Spanish, and full details for publishers.

The Nueva Trova

La canción de la Nueva Trova (The Song of the Nueva Trova)

Atril Ediciones Musicales · 2001

A history of the movement told with the voices of its founders, from its first public appearance in February 1967. The author's first book, born as his university thesis.

See the book

Trovadoras (Women of the Trova)

Editorial Oriente · 2008

Working women songwriters on the place they, and the women before them, have held in Cuban song, with the gender scholar Isabel Moya and the musicographer Lino Betancourt.

See the book

Convertida en canción (Turned into Song)

Editorial Capiro · 2019

Which woman are Cuban troubadours singing to? A reading of the Nueva Trova's lyrics on women in public life, sexuality, sex work, infidelity and homosexual love.

See the book

Poetry

Trampas, retratos y un 17 rojo (Traps, Portraits and a Red 17)

Editorial de la Mujer · 2005

Three poets, three forms: free verse by María del Carmen Mestas, haiku by Marcel Luerio, and a sequence of erotic décimas by the author, Retratos de mañana. His only poetry collection to date.

See the book

Fiction

Las guerreras de la luz (The Warriors of the Light)

Editorial de la Mujer · 2011 · Premio La Rosa Blanca 2012, UNEAC

A sorcerer from the far reaches of darkness tries to halt all life on a quiet island. A girl, Aria Musa, her friends and a magical hummingbird raise an army to fight him. The author's first novel.

See the book

El Escudo de Valnúss (The Shield of Valnúss)

Editorial de la Mujer · 2015

A sorceress, an undersea warrior, an enchanted steed and a corsair join forces against a sorcerer who has sown chaos in their worlds. Five books, one novel, published as an illustrated boxed set.

See the book

El otro lado del espejo (The Other Side of the Mirror)

Editorial Gente Nueva · 2017 · Honourable mention, La Edad de Oro 2014

Could those who live on the other side of the mirror invade us? One of the last wizards of the Library travels across times and magical dimensions to save his love. Heroic fantasy in homage to Borges.

See the book

Grimorium

Editorial Oriente · 2018

Three parallel stories, set in different times, whose protagonists pursue the same forbidden book and its ancient, terrifying mystery. Horror built on atmosphere, in homage to Lovecraft.

See the book

Perdidos en un librero (Lost in a Bookcase)

Editorial Quisicuaba · 2026

A wizard's hat, a fairy's wand, a horse, a doll and a scientist zombie get lost inside the books of a magic bookcase, and end up in the pages of Cuban mythology. The author's newest book.

See the book

De la extraña aventura de Don Quijote por lejanas tierras y otros sucesos hasta hoy olvidados (Of the Strange Adventure of Don Quixote in Distant Lands)

Editorial Cubaliteraria · 2021 · First prize, Reescribir El Quijote en Cuba, 2005

Swept up by a gale at the windmills of chapter eight, Don Quixote and Sancho Panza land in a Caribbean capital in the 1990s, where a young poet takes them in. The jury that awarded it included Leonardo Padura. Free to read in Spanish.

See the book

Collective volumes

Anthologies and shared volumes that include texts by the author. Rights offered here cover only what he wrote, never the complete volume.

En un lugar de Cuba de cuyo nombre quiero acordarme (Somewhere in Cuba, Whose Name I Do Wish to Recall)

Diputación de Córdoba · 2005 · First prize, 2005

The anthology of the competition held for the four hundredth anniversary of Don Quixote, published in Spain, where the author won first prize from a jury that included Leonardo Padura.

See the book

Nota de prensa y otros minicuentos (Press Release and Other Very Short Stories)

Editorial Cajachina · 2006

Flash fiction from the El Dinosaurio competition, which in 2006 drew 1,212 entries from Cuba and abroad, as far as Australia. It includes the author's Sin segundas partes.

See the book

Vamos a cantar y a soñar (Let's Sing and Dream)

Editorial de la Mujer · 2008

Texts, songs, games and poems for children, compiled by Nerys Pupo. Three poems by the author, one of which later gave birth to two characters of his children's books.

See the book

Cuentos de muñecas (¡A leer y a jugar!) (Doll Stories)

Editorial de la Mujer · 2015

Stories and poems for children with dolls as their protagonists, compiled by Magaly Sánchez Ochoa. The author's first children's story, which gave birth to the characters of his later books.

See the book

Rights

World rights for editions and translations are available outside Cuba, where the author's publishers keep their exclusivity. Full manuscripts and synopses are available on request.
