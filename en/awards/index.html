Awards

The prizes the craft has left along the way. I don't write for them, but when they come, they are welcome.

2026

First Prize in Poetry, XXX Premio Farraluque de Literatura Erótica

For Tres delirios y un desnudo, which can be read here in full. At the same competition, an honourable mention in short fiction for Revelaciones.

2026

Premio Colateral Yasmina Calcines, XXVI Concurso Nacional Ala Décima

For Sonata de la lluvia, which can be read here in full, at the poetry competition organised by the Grupo Ala Décima and the Centro Iberoamericano de la Décima y el Verso Improvisado.

2014

Honourable mention, Concurso La Edad de Oro

From Editorial Gente Nueva, for the heroic fantasy novel El otro lado del espejo, then unpublished.

2014

Finalist, Concurso de Minicuentos El Dinosaurio 2013

Organised by the Onelio Jorge Cardoso Centre for Literary Training, for Proclama Real, written in 2009. The result was announced in 2014.

2013

Premio La Rosa Blanca 2012 for Best Text

Awarded by the Children's and Young People's Literature Section of the UNEAC Writers' Association, for the novel Las guerreras de la luz.

2005

First Prize, Reescribir El Quijote en Cuba

Sponsored by the Embassy of Spain in Cuba, for De la extraña aventura de Don Quijote por lejanas tierras y otros sucesos hasta hoy olvidados, published in the anthology En un lugar de Cuba de cuyo nombre quiero acordarme and, in 2021, as an e-book.

2002

Prize in Written Press, Concurso 26 de Julio

From the Union of Cuban Journalists (UPEC), at Editorial de la Mujer, for the article Digo que yo no soy un hombre puro.

I have also received recognitions for work awarded at national press festivals, and a laurel or two must still be lost among my papers.
