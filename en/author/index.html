The author

Antonio López Sánchez. Havana, 1973. Everything he has written, in one place.

In short

Antonio López Sánchez was born in Havana on 16 January 1973. He holds a degree in Social Communication from the University of Havana, graduated from the ninth course of the Onelio Jorge Cardoso Centre for Literary Training in 2007, and completed the postgraduate programme Crítica con Arte at the José Martí International Institute of Journalism in 2022.

He has published fourteen books since 2001, across heroic fantasy, horror, children's literature, poetry, and essays and interviews on the Cuban Nueva Trova. He has spent his working life in the Cuban cultural press, and he lives and writes in Alamar, the Havana district facing the sea.

This page is the index of the whole body of work: the books, the journalism, the stories, the poems and the novels still looking for a publisher. Every entry links to the page where it can be read in full.

Everything here is in Spanish

The whole of Antonio López Sánchez's work is written in Spanish, and none of it has been translated yet. There is no English edition of any book, and no translated sample: what this site offers a publisher is the original, complete and ready to send.

We are open to offers to translate any part of it, into any language: a single book, the whole fiction, the three volumes on the Nueva Trova, the poetry, the stories or the unpublished novels. Rights for editions and translations outside Cuba are free, and the enquiry goes to the author's literary representation.

The journalism

Twenty-two signed pieces, from a 1994 interview with the singer-songwriter Carlos Varela to a 2021 piece on electric guitars in the trova. Interviews, book reviews and essays, almost all of them with music nearby.

These pages exist only in Spanish. The line under each title says what it is about.

Interviews

  • El riesgo de buscar Proyecto Voces, School of Communication, University of Havana · 1994 A 1994 conversation with the singer-songwriter Carlos Varela, just after the album Como los peces. It stayed unpublished for thirty years: the magazine that commissioned it never appeared.
  • Colores al desnudo Revista Muchacha · December 2005 The painter on the palette knife he found while looking for something else, on faces that are neither male nor female, and on what he steals from Van Gogh, from Lam and from his own father.
  • Rebeldes con causa Revista Muchacha · November 2005 An afternoon at the Patio de María with the leader of Hipnosis, a metal band with three women in it, in a scene where women are scarce.
  • Palabras desde una flauta Revista Muchacha · June 2009 During an interview with a flautist, a small miracle happens: it is her flute that answers the questions.
  • La trova canta a dos lentes La Jiribilla · March 2010 Two photographers, Alain Gutiérrez and Kaloian Santos, explain how you photograph a sound.

Reviews

  • Locuras de pincel June 2014 El Loquito, the cartoon character created by René de la Nuez, returns in an anthology by Axel Li, and with him a whole line of Cuban graphic humour that said what could not be said.
  • ¿El verde de la desesperanza? August 2014 A novel by Marvelys Marrero enters childhood without sweetening it. Do its paths lead to hopelessness?
  • Para sonar en buen cubano A review of a book of interviews with major Cuban musicians, first published in La Gaceta de Cuba. From Juan Formell to Orishas.
  • A velo descubierto las palabras June 2014 Helen Hernández Hormilla interviews several Cuban women writers. Among other subjects, at length: what feminism is and is not.
  • Una historia de un siglo March 2014 Leonardo Acosta travels a hundred years of jazz on the island. A vital book for understanding, and enjoying, the history of that music in Cuba.
  • Una isla de crimen y enigma June 2014 An anthology by Leopoldo Luis and Rafael Grillo tries to give the island back the crime story that the Special Period left without magazines.
  • ¡Salud, maestro H. Zumbado! May 2013 A review written as if it were a television programme, for a master of humour who deserved that joke.
  • Sonando la lira y el bongó May 2013 Carpentier's La música en Cuba returns to the bookshops with the final chapter the author himself had removed.

Chronicles and essays

  • Trovar con los pies y bailar con la cabeza La Jiribilla · December 2012 On the birthday of Los Van Van, a curious chapter of Juan Formell's work: is he a trovador with an orchestra?
  • En su primer medio vinilo La Jiribilla · June 2012 Fifty years of the singer-songwriter, told record by record.
  • Trova eléctrica (y fregada) La Jiribilla · September 2021 A list made while washing up: the electric guitars that found their way into Cuban trova, from the GES to Habana Abierta.
  • Los diez (y tantos) discos de trova que debes escuchar AM:PM Which records to put on for someone just arriving at Cuban trova, knowing the list is going to annoy somebody.

The stories

Eleven complete stories, each with a page of its own. Some come from published books, some from books still with their publisher, some belong to nobody yet.

  • La muñeca y el príncipe (The Doll and the Prince) A dragon has appeared in Pilar's toy room. But it will take a prince to face it.
  • Aquelarre (Coven) Six very special friends must complete an arcane rite to survive in today's world.
  • Asesinato (Murder) The downstairs neighbour was afraid of a tree. So she decided to kill it...
  • Clínica del miedo (Clinic of Fear) A patient breaks down on the couch of a most unusual specialist.
  • Duelo (Duel) Two figures face each other in the snow. A duel between unequal opponents...
  • Luces (Lights) A writer caught between power cuts tries to make literature and keep up with the housework.
  • Sombreros, pamelas y mágicas varitas (Hats, Sun Hats and Magic Wands) Brujito Sombrero is woken by a sonata, and on the other side of the fence a magic wand goes by.
  • Cantar el cuento (III) (Singing the Story (III)) Part of a trilogy where the stories sound like songs too. Adolescent love and its passions, sung between two bodies.
  • La urna del tío (The Uncle's Urn) A vivid portrait of life and its turns, told by the ashes of a dead man.
  • Traje de lluvia (A Dress of Rain) Rain calls up dreams and literature at once, and lets you live inside them.
  • Hipo-Tálamo (Hypothalamus, and the marriage bed) A journey through a child's mind, its ambivalences, and a horror that may be real or imagined.

The unpublished novels

Four finished novels that have not yet found a publisher. Each has its synopsis, the author's own note and the excerpts he chose. The complete manuscripts can be sent the same day they are asked for.

  • Preguntas. Un día en la vida de Juan Pérez Novel A single day can fit in a novel. Even if, at times, the questions overflow.
  • Mis diarios de cama Novel · for adult readers A woman's diary, with her duties, her loves, her sorrows. A novel told in the first person by its own protagonist.
  • Sonidos Horror novel The search for the perfect sound breeds a murderer in the nineteenth century, and unleashes a story of horrors and deaths that will echo into the present, and perhaps the future.
  • Palabras Fantasy novel A pursuit across several dimensions leads to the Library. Past and present will cross their lines. Ambition will crave power. Minutes may be centuries, but love will remain untouched.
