Stories

Contarte: the room where the stories live.

Once upon a time...

Those ancient words announce a story. Some of my stories live here, each with a place of its own and a hand held out, inviting you in.

Some are free, still unattached. Some come from books waiting to see the light. Some are cheerful, others dark. But all of them are ready to keep you company.

The story, that sprite that has kept humanity company since the dawn of time, still gives away magic, realities and even fears.

The stories are published in their original Spanish, as all literary texts on this site are.
