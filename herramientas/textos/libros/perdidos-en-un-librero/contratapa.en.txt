This book offers a very special journey. A wizard's hat, a fairy's wand, a horse, a doll and a scientist zombie get lost among the volumes of a magic bookcase. With the help of a cricket captain and his crew, our friends must face some villains who, in the pages of Cuban mythology, have kidnapped a pond and its Madre de Aguas, the water serpent of Cuban legend. Will you join us on these magical adventures?
