Which woman are our troubadours singing to? Have the women reflected in those sung portraits changed, or grown? The Cuban Nueva Trova, in constant dialogue with society, has offered through its work reflections, opinions and positions on many of our most important collective debates. While love has been the workhorse among the themes of trova songs, other subjects have also come under the artistic gaze of this creative current.
The analysis of phenomena such as women's place in society, individual and couple sexuality, prostitution, break-ups, infidelity or homosexual love, to name only a few examples where women are always present, has had its place in trova lyrics, and is dissected in these pages. These are, moreover, songs whose value lies not only in their ethical positions and their arguments, but in the very high artistic, musical and literary quality with which they have taken on such subjects.
