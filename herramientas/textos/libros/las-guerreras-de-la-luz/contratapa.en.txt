An evil sorcerer, come from the cosmic reaches of the shadows, tries to halt all life and all work on a quiet island, so as to establish the rule of darkness and seize the world for the forces of night. With the help of Albor, a brave and magical hummingbird sent by the light, the very young Aria Musa and her friends must face great adventures before raising an army by force of sword and effort and, now warriors of the light, fighting the enemy sorcerer and his terrible henchmen.
