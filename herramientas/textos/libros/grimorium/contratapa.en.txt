If you enter this book you must be ready to risk everything, to live through everything and, above all, to sink into something accursed. Because the Grimorium is waiting for you. Once you start reading it, you will not be able to put it down; only go forward, reach the end and become part of a terrible outcome. At every step you will find tension, adventure, suspense and a sea of dread.
Here you will be accompanied by three parallel stories, set in different times, whose protagonists pursue the same goal: to learn more about a text supposedly forbidden to men and women for centuries, one that hides an ancient and terrifying mystery.
After these recommendations, there are no options left but to open this book or to flee from its story. One last warning: if you go in, you will not be able to escape these pages. The Grimorium will have caught you.
