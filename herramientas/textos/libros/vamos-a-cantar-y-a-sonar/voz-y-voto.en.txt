During the years I worked as a journalist at Editorial de la Mujer, I was close to more than one of the house's publishing projects. One of them was this anthology of writing for children, which brings together texts, songs, games and poems. At the express invitation of its author, the writer and compiler Nerys Pupo, this volume publishes my poems Tus ojos, Náufrago and Trovada de Grillo y Polimita.
From the last of these, especially, two characters would be born years later. They are the protagonists of one of the love-and-adventure stories that make up one of the children's sagas I have written and, fortunately, already published. Captain Gallardo Grillo and the snail Polymita Yara have their most distant origin in that poem.
