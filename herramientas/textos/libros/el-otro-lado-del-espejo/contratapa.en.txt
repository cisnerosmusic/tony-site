Could those who live on the other side of the mirror invade us? Could that invasion upset the course of things, and change the future? Faced with such possibilities, Adiur Lugh, one of the last wizards of the Library, will use all his powers as a Copyist to save his love for the time to come. The protagonist must travel from one Channel to another, across different times and magical dimensions, to find powerful relics and fight his enemies. But on his way he will meet hard obstacles, labyrinths, riddles and battles, which will test the strength of his sword as much as that of his mind and his heart.
Inspired by a quotation from the Argentine Jorge Luis Borges, and using in homage some of the phrases, ideas and objects that author created, this heroic fantasy novel is woven. A tale in which love and goodness are the engines that lead to taking up sacrifice and struggle.
Antonio López Sánchez (Havana, 1973) holds a degree in Social Communication from the University of Havana. Journalist, poet and fiction writer, he graduated from the Onelio Jorge Cardoso Centre for Literary Training in 2007. His published books include El Escudo de Valnúss (Editorial de la Mujer, 2015), Las guerreras de la luz (Editorial de la Mujer, 2011), Trovadoras (Editorial Oriente, 2008) and La canción de la Nueva Trova (Atril Ediciones Musicales, 2001), among other works.
