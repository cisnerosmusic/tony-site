   Her true sex hidden under men's clothes, Zoria Zylad of Valnúss must face the prohibitions of the temple of Adar in order to develop her powers as a sorceress. Yet for all her father's help and the valuable friendships she finds along the way, a terrible future is closing in on the Land of Reul. Evil is about to break loose, and the real intentions of a baleful enemy, the powerful sorcerer Soltrán Sauort, will come to the surface.
