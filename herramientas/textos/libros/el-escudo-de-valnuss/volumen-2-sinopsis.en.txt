   Born in the sunken city of Aglas, the daughter of a milk-whale milker, the strong-willed Sedna Laiacor ignores prejudice and custom and takes on enormous challenges in pursuit of her greatest dream. But the harsh, cruel trials to become one of the hundred women of the army's elite corps, the Gilara warriors who guard the cities of the Land of Verne, will not be the only danger she has to face.
