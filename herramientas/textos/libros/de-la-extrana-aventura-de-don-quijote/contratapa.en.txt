For unknown reasons, Don Quixote and his inseparable Sancho Panza, just as they charge the famous windmills of chapter eight of the original novel, are caught up by a gale and land in the capital of a Caribbean island, some time in the 1990s. A young poet takes the eminent knight into his home and, armed with magic and poetry, does everything he can to right that wrong.
Such is the beginning of this singular adventure story, which won First Prize in the Reescribir El Quijote en Cuba competition, held in 2005 by the Spanish Agency for International Cooperation and the Embassy of Spain in Cuba. Now Editorial Cubaliteraria publishes it for the first time on the island, in digital form, as an EPUB.
