From a home that no longer exists, two civilizations pursue a fugitive, waging war on each other and destroying dimensions as they go. The davolas and the andaras follow Master Astrom to Earth. But this attempt at capture and invasion meets an insurmountable obstacle in the Earth's atmosphere, and it also awakens a hunger for the secret arts guarded by the Library. Terrible mysteries lie hidden there, and powerful forces of creation, but also of death. Yet it is love that will embody, in those it chooses, the true battle for life.
Several protagonists, in different places and times that sooner or later must converge, will try to seize these extraordinary secrets or fight to keep the powers of the Demiurge out of evil hands. A story of fantasy and love that unfolds over centuries and will seek its resolution by travelling from the present into the past.
