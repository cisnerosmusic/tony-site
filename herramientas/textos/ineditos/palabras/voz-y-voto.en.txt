This novel has a few internal keys I would like to reveal. In a way it is a kind of prequel to my book El otro lado del espejo. It is not a first part, and one need not read either to understand the other, but there are many connecting threads between them. The Library that appears in that earlier book, with its immense corridors and its mirrors, owes a clear debt to the Argentine writer Jorge Luis Borges. Even so, discovering a possible origin for that place, or at least an earlier stage of it, and telling of its world, those in charge of it, its pupils and its masters, could also make an interesting fable, one worth telling. This new novel tries to answer those questions, or at least some of them.
So, out of a love story (that feeling is always, always close by), new characters appear here, and new games with time and space. Because it is a relatively short work, just over a hundred pages, it may leave doors open for returning to its central ideas. Perhaps not with the same characters, but with that same axis of the powerful and much coveted Library, its guardian, and some other love or fantasy still to be resolved. So there may well be another novel born to follow that path. For now, waiting for its pages, this one remains.
