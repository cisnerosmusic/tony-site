A very wealthy English nobleman of the nineteenth century, gifted with a truly unique and exceptional ear, becomes obsessed with finding the perfect sound. Far from turning his gift into a lasting creative act, it is the sounds of death and pain that awaken his deepest emotions and pleasures. His search also leads to the discovery of a disembodied entity, which offers the Englishman access to an entire universe made of sound, in exchange for a sinister offering of lives and, afterwards, the sacrifice of our whole world. Revenge, unrequited love and ambition will drive him to acts of extreme inhumanity.
In the present, a strange fire at an auction house and a demonic invocation spoken in Latin, which appears inexplicably in the recording studios of a music producer, unleash dangerous mysteries. The producer and her sceptical friend, a journalist, terrified by what has happened, turn to an expert in paranormal sound. Then an exorcist enters the story, and a very singular and mysterious cardinal, retired from his religious duties, who lives under protection in a discreet villa. An enormous danger ends up drawing all of them into a violent conflict, where the lines of past, present and future come together to decide the fate of humanity itself.
